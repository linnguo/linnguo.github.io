Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android game Fillful: Block Puzzle · Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy explains what information is and is not handled when you play Fillful: Block Puzzle (the “Game”). It is written to match the data practices we declare on Google Play.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other information that directly identifies you.

Your game progress (unlocked levels, best move counts, settings, and whether the solution preview has been unlocked) is stored only on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the Game. We cannot see it.

2. Information processed by the advertising SDK

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob (interstitial ads every few levels, and optional rewarded videos you may choose to watch). To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID (a resettable identifier);
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language, and general settings;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address (country / region level only);
  • ad interaction data (which ads were requested, shown, or clicked) used for delivery, frequency capping, and reporting.

We do not receive or store this information ourselves. Google’s handling of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. The Game does not include any other analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

3. Your choices and consent

  • Personalized ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalized advertising, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device).
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad-related data is collected you are shown a consent message from Google’s User Messaging Platform. Advertising is limited to what your choice allows. You may change your choice by clearing the Game’s data in Android Settings → Apps → Fillful → Storage → Clear data; the consent message will be shown again on the next launch.

4. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the advertising SDK, please contact us and we will help you exercise the choices above.

5. Data retention and security

We keep no server-side database of players, so there is no personal data for us to retain or delete. Data collected by the advertising SDK is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and retained by Google according to its own policies.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be reflected in the Google Play listing.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: linn.guo@qq.com
